# GEIT LTD — WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT MASTER PROMPT

## Project identity

Build a premium, modern, responsive corporate website for **Global Energy Innovation Technologies Ltd (GEIT Ltd)**, a Rwanda-based energy consulting, advisory, project-development and technology company.

The website must position GEIT as an engineering-led energy intelligence company combining rigorous technical analysis, commercial insight, and practical AI-powered digital solutions.

## 1. Company information

- Legal name: Global Energy Innovation Technologies Ltd
- Short name: GEIT Ltd
- Country: Rwanda
- Head office: Kigali, Rwanda
- Registration / TIN: 157099400
- Founders and shareholders: Dr. Hyacinthe M. Nshuti (50%) and Mr. Noel Dushimiyimana (50%)
- Initial market: Rwanda
- Expansion strategy: East Africa, wider Africa, and international clients
- Sector: Energy consulting, advisory, project development, carbon-footprint services and digital energy solutions.

Use company registration details only where appropriate, such as the legal footer or corporate information page.

## 2. Brand positioning

Vision: To be a leading African energy innovation company, helping organizations make smarter, cleaner and more cost-effective energy decisions.

Mission: To provide independent, data-driven energy consulting, project development and digital technology solutions that reduce energy costs, improve reliability, accelerate renewable-energy adoption, measure and reduce greenhouse-gas emissions, and support the energy transition.

Core values:
- Technical rigor
- Independence
- Measurable impact
- Integrity
- Safety and compliance
- Innovation
- African relevance
- Credible carbon accounting

Brand promise: Energy decisions should be driven by data, rigorous analysis and sound economics.

Suggested tagline: "Energy Intelligence. Measurable Impact."

## 3. Target clients

Design the website for:
- Commercial property owners and facility managers
- Industrial and manufacturing companies
- Businesses seeking lower energy costs
- Renewable-energy developers and project owners
- Solar PV and battery-storage investors
- Electric-fleet and EV-charging operators
- Financial institutions and infrastructure investors
- Public institutions and development organizations

Prioritize the Rwandan market while making the brand credible for East African and international clients.

## 4. Service portfolio

Create a scalable service architecture covering all eleven services:

1. Energy Management
2. Energy Consulting
3. Energy Project Development
4. Energy Market Research
5. Energy Technology Solutions
6. Renewable Energy Advisory
7. Energy Efficiency & Energy Auditing
8. Battery Energy Storage & Energy Flexibility Consulting
9. EV Charging Infrastructure Advisory
10. AI-Powered Energy Analytics & Digital Energy Solutions
11. Carbon Footprint Services

Each service must have a dedicated, search-engine-friendly page or route.

Each service page should explain:
- The client's problem
- What GEIT provides
- Typical scope of work
- Expected deliverables
- The engagement process
- Who benefits
- A relevant call to action

Do not promise specific savings, investment returns, certifications or emissions reductions without verified evidence.

## 6. Visual design

Create a premium corporate identity using:

- Deep navy: #10243A
- Energy green: #168568
- Light grey: #F2F5F7
- White: #FFFFFF
- Amber accent: #E8B64B

Use a modern sans-serif font, generous whitespace, strong typographic hierarchy, clear layouts, subtle motion and high-quality energy-infrastructure imagery.

Visual themes can include:
- Solar PV and electrical infrastructure
- Commercial and industrial energy systems
- Battery energy storage
- Electricity networks
- Building-energy monitoring

Avoid generic AI robot imagery, excessive gradients, clutter, unnecessary animation and the appearance of a basic solar-installation contractor website.

The design must communicate technical credibility, independence, innovation and African market relevance.

## 8. Contact and lead generation

Create a professional consultation request form with:
- Full name
- Organization
- Business email
- Telephone, optional
- Country
- Service of interest
- Project description
- Preferred contact method, optional
- Privacy-policy consent

Include appropriate validation, accessible error messages, loading states, submission confirmation and failure handling.

Protect the form against spam and validate submitted data on the server.

Never expose secret API keys in frontend code.

Do not display a success message unless the enquiry has actually been accepted by the backend.

- SEO metadata, canonical URLs and sitemap
- Open Graph metadata

## 10. Content and integrity rules

Use the company information supplied in this prompt as the source of truth.

Do not invent:
- Client names or logos
- Testimonials
- Completed projects
- Staff credentials beyond those supplied
- Regulatory approvals
- Professional certifications
- Partnerships
- Awards
- Financial results
- Measured energy savings
- Emissions-reduction figures

Where real case studies are not available, use clearly labelled illustrative examples or explain the typical project methodology.

Do not claim that GEIT is certified to ISO 50001 or accredited as an energy auditor unless this has been verified.

Avoid presenting proposed services as completed projects.

Structure the website so that GEIT can later add:

- Multi-site energy dashboards
- AI-based performance analytics
- Carbon-accounting software
- Customer accounts and role-based access

## 12. Working method

Before implementation:
1. Inspect the existing repository and identify its framework and current state.
2. Present the proposed site architecture and design direction.
3. Identify missing assets and configuration.
4. Propose an implementation sequence.

Then implement the website incrementally.

Do not overwrite existing files blindly. Preserve useful code and explain major changes.

After each phase:
- List files created or changed.
- Explain the features implemented.
- Run relevant linting, type checks and build tests.
- Fix errors before moving forward.
- Identify any configuration or credentials I must provide.

Begin with the architecture and design system, then implement the homepage and shared components. Continue with service pages, contact functionality, SEO, testing and deployment preparation.

The final result should look and feel like a serious international energy advisory and technology company headquartered in Rwanda.
