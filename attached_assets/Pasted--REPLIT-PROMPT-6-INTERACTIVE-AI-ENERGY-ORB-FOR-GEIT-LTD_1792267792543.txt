# REPLIT PROMPT 6 — INTERACTIVE AI ENERGY ORB FOR GEIT LTD

Act as a senior creative developer specializing in immersive web experiences, WebGL, Three.js, React, motion design and high-performance interactive interfaces.

Upgrade the existing Global Energy Innovation Technologies Ltd (GEIT Ltd) website with a distinctive, premium animated visual experience.

## 1. Creative concept

Create a dark, living AI interface in which a cloud of mint-green and violet light breathes as a luminous orb, expands into a spiral galaxy, and gathers itself into a glowing artificial-intelligence brain.

The animation should communicate:
- Energy and transformation
- Intelligence and computation
- Scientific innovation
- Digital energy systems
- The convergence of engineering and AI

It should feel sophisticated, cinematic and technically credible, not like a game or a science-fiction movie poster.

## 2. Animation sequence

Create a continuous, seamless animation with these visual phases:

**Phase A — Breathing energy orb**

A luminous spherical cloud of mint-green, turquoise and violet particles floats in a dark environment. The orb slowly expands and contracts, with volumetric-looking light, fine particles and subtle electrical filaments.

**Phase B — Spiral galaxy**

The orb opens outward into a rotating spiral galaxy. Thousands of particles form elegant spiral arms around a bright central core. The galaxy rotates slowly and sheds fine luminous particles.

**Phase C — AI brain formation**

The spiral galaxy gathers inward. Particles converge into a recognizable, symmetrical three-dimensional human brain silhouette made from light, interconnected neural nodes and flowing electrical pathways.

The brain pulses gently, and selected neural connections illuminate in response to interaction.

**Phase D — Continuous transformation**

The brain gradually disperses into a spiral galaxy and returns to the original energy orb. The transitions must be fluid and continuous, without sudden jumps, flashes or obvious loading gaps.

The entire sequence should loop seamlessly.

## 3. Interactive behavior

Make the experience respond to the user:

- Mouse movement gently influences the orb's orientation and surrounding particles.
- Hovering over the visual increases particle activity and illuminates neural connections.
- Clicking or tapping the visual triggers a subtle pulse of light.
- Scrolling can gently influence animation progress if it can be implemented without harming performance.
- On touch devices, use appropriate touch interactions without requiring hover.
- Respect the user's reduced-motion accessibility preference.

Do not make the entire website follow the mouse or create excessive motion that interferes with reading.

## 4. Visual direction

Use:
- Deep navy and near-black backgrounds
- Mint green: #8FFFD2
- Turquoise: #42E8D5
- Violet: #9B72FF
- Subtle white highlights
- Soft bloom and restrained glow
- Fine particles and delicate neural lines

The scene should have depth, elegant lighting, a restrained color palette and premium scientific visualization aesthetics.

Avoid excessive neon, large distracting flashes, cartoon brains, stock illustrations and generic AI imagery.

## 5. Placement on the GEIT website

Integrate the animation into the homepage hero or a dedicated AI and Digital Energy Solutions section.

The hero must retain the existing GEIT identity:
- Official GEIT Ltd logo
- Company name: Global Energy Innovation Technologies
- Tagline: "Energy Intelligence. Measurable Impact."
- Main headline: "Make smarter energy decisions. Build a more efficient, resilient and low-carbon future."
- Primary CTA: "Request a Consultation"
- Secondary CTA: "Explore Our Services"

Keep the headline and calls to action readable. Position the animation beside the text on desktop, or as a restrained background or stacked visual on mobile.

Do not replace the official company logo with the animated orb.

Keep the existing website navigation, service pages, contact section, footer and other functionality intact.

## 6. Technical implementation

Inspect the existing project before choosing the implementation.

Prefer a performant React-based solution using Three.js or React Three Fiber if compatible with the existing stack.

Use GPU-accelerated particles and shader-based effects where appropriate. Do not introduce multiple overlapping graphics libraries without a clear reason.

Create a reusable component, for example:

`components/ai-energy-orb.tsx`

Separate the animation logic from the homepage layout.

Requirements:
- Responsive sizing
- Efficient rendering
- Proper cleanup of animation loops, event listeners, geometries and materials
- Lazy loading where appropriate
- Graceful fallback when WebGL is unavailable
- Reduced-motion support
- Mobile performance safeguards
- No unnecessary API calls
- No paid external services
- No requirement for a database
- No exposed credentials

Do not generate thousands of individual DOM elements for the particles. Use an appropriate GPU-rendered approach.

## 7. Quality and performance

The animation must enhance the website rather than slow it down.

Use adaptive particle counts and rendering quality based on device capability. Pause or reduce animation work when the page is not visible.

On mobile, reduce visual complexity where needed.

If a realistic animated brain would require an external 3D model, do not invent a model URL or depend on an unreliable external asset. Instead, build a recognizable stylized brain from procedural particle geometry and branching neural connections.

Ensure that the animation loads progressively and never blocks the navigation or primary calls to action.

## 8. Implementation and testing

First inspect the current homepage and identify the best integration point.

Implement the animation without rebuilding unrelated parts of the website.

Then:
1. Run the application.
2. Verify that the orb, galaxy and brain states are distinguishable.
3. Check that the transformations are smooth.
4. Test mouse and touch interaction.
5. Test desktop and mobile layouts.
6. Check reduced-motion behavior and the fallback.
7. Run available linting, type checks and production build.
8. Fix errors and report any limitations.

Do not claim that realistic three-dimensional transformations work unless they have been implemented and verified.

The final result should feel like a signature visual identity for an African energy innovation and AI company: intelligent, elegant, immersive and professionally engineered.
