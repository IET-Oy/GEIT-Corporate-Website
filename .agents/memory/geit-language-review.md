description: Owner-approved draft translation rollout and the human-review boundary.
---

The owner chose “Prepare complete drafts labelled as awaiting human review” rather than supplying human-reviewed translations for the GEIT website.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved that approach when no human-reviewed full-page translations were available.

**How to apply:** Draft French, Kinyarwanda and Swahili content may be shown with a clear review-pending notice and access to the English source. Do not describe generated translations as human-reviewed or remove the notice without actual human approval.

The owner subsequently said “human review approved” for the public FAQ and support translations in French, Kinyarwanda and Swahili.

**Why:** The owner explicitly confirmed human review when asked for feedback on the side-by-side support review packet. No reviewer identity or corrections were provided.

**How to apply:** Treat that confirmation as limited to FAQ/support copy. Do not extend it to the other website content or legal translations; their draft-review requirements remain in force.
