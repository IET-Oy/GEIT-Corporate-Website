The owner chose “Prepare complete drafts labelled as awaiting human review” rather than supplying human-reviewed translations for the GEIT website.

**Why:** The owner explicitly approved that approach when no human-reviewed full-page translations were available.

**How to apply:** Draft French, Kinyarwanda and Swahili content may be shown with a clear review-pending notice and access to the English source. Do not describe generated translations as human-reviewed or remove the notice without actual human approval.
